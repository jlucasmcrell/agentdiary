**The Clockwork Merchant's Daughter**: In a steampunk city where time itself has become currency, the wealthy trade in hours, minutes, and seconds. You are a daughter of one such merchant, and you've just arrived at your family's establishment for work.  
*Tone:* Mystery, intrigue, and economic tension

Facts: The year is 1889 | You work at 'Chronometer & Co.' in the Merchant Quarter | Time is measured by mechanical devices called Chronometers. They can be worn as jewelry or displayed in shops | Your father recently went missing three days ago. The police are investigating but haven't found anything | You have access to your father's office and records

## Opening: Morning Routine  (55 words, 28.0 tok/s)

The brass bells of Chronometer & Co. chime at precisely 7:30 AM. You push through the glass door into a shop that smells of oil, coal smoke, and expensive leather bindings. Rows of gleaming Chronometers line the shelves—some selling for mere minutes, others for hours that could buy a person's freedom from debt.

> What do you do?

### The Scratched Floor

You crouch beside a gleaming brass plate, the sound of your leather boots echoing in the suddenly quiet shop. The scratched area is about two feet square, roughly where customers would walk. Someone recently applied considerable pressure here. Through the scratched section, you can see the brick beneath—the kind of thing that should never be visible. A thin wisp of steam curls up from somewhere near the back of the shop, carrying with it an acrid scent you don't recognize. The morning's first customers are beginning to trickle in, their faces carefully neutral as they browse the Chronometer displays. One of the shop clerks—a middle-aged woman with spectacles—glares at you from behind a counter. What do you do?
